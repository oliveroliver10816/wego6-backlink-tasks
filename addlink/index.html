Add the missing link

The 12 articles are published and they are good. The link to our website is missing on all 12. This job adds it. It is copy and paste only.

The same 7 steps on every page

  1. Open the page address from the card.
  2. Click Edit (the pencil button, bottom right).
  3. Click on the last line of the article, at the very end of the text.
  4. Press Enter once. This makes a new empty line.
  5. Click Copy the sentence on the card. Then press Ctrl and V together to paste.
  6. Click Publish.
  7. Check it. Look at the new sentence. Some words must be blue. Click them. They must open our website.

The link is already inside the sentence you copy. You do not have to make the link yourself. When you paste, the blue words come with it.

Two things to be careful about

1. Do not delete anything. You are only adding one sentence at the end. Do not change the title. Do not change the article. Do not remove words.

2. If the words are not blue after you publish, tell me. Do not try to fix it yourself. Just write "not blue" next to that page in the Google Sheet and go to the next page.

The small signs like # and * in some articles are fine. Leave them. Do not spend time on them.
